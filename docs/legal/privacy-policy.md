# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** December 25, 2024

**Effective Date:** December 25, 2024

## Introduction

STRMR ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Android TV application ("STRMR" or the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

**Google Account Information**
- Email address
- Display name
- Profile photo URL
- Google account identifier

This information is collected when you sign in with your Google account, which is required to use the App.

**Third-Party Service Credentials**
When you choose to connect third-party services, we store:
- Trakt.tv: OAuth tokens for syncing watchlist and viewing history
- Debrid services (Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, Premiumize): API tokens for premium link resolution

### Information Collected Automatically

**Usage Data**
- App version and device information
- Feature usage patterns
- Error logs and crash reports (if Firebase is enabled)

**Local Storage**
- Viewing preferences and settings
- Cached content metadata
- Playback history (stored locally on device)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

1. **Provide Core Functionality**
   - Authenticate your identity via Google Sign-In
   - Sync your watchlist and viewing history with Trakt.tv
   - Enable premium streaming through debrid services

2. **Improve the App**
   - Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
   - Diagnose and fix technical issues
   - Develop new features based on user needs

3. **Ensure Security**
   - Verify app authenticity
   - Prevent unauthorized access
   - Enforce minimum version requirements

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- User preferences and tokens are stored locally on your device
- Sensitive tokens are encrypted using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences
- Database information is stored in Android's protected app storage

### Cloud Storage
- We do not operate our own servers for user data storage
- Your data synced with Trakt.tv is subject to [Trakt's Privacy Policy](https://trakt.tv/privacy)
- Debrid service data is subject to each provider's respective privacy policy

### Security Measures
- All network communications use HTTPS encryption
- OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication flows
- Tokens are stored using AES-256 encryption

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share information only in the following circumstances:
- **With your consent:** When you explicitly authorize sharing
- **With service providers:** Third-party services you choose to connect (Trakt, debrid services)
- **Legal requirements:** When required by law or to protect our rights

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Control
- View your stored data in the App's settings
- Disconnect third-party services at any time
- Sign out to clear local authentication data

### Data Deletion
- Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data
- To delete data from third-party services, visit their respective platforms
- Contact us to request deletion of any data we may have

### Opt-Out
- You may decline to connect optional services (Trakt, debrid)
- Core functionality requires Google Sign-In

## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
