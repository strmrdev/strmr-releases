# STRMR

A modern Android TV streaming application for discovering and watching movies and TV shows. STRMR integrates with popular media databases and debrid services to provide a seamless viewing experience on your TV.

## Features

- **Content Discovery**: Browse trending, popular, and curated lists of movies and TV shows
- **Trakt Integration**: Sync your watchlist, collection, and watch history with Trakt.tv
- **Continue Watching**: Resume playback from where you left off across devices
- **Multiple Debrid Services**: Support for Premiumize, Real-Debrid, and AllDebrid
- **Subtitle Support**: Automatic subtitle fetching via OpenSubtitles
- **Customizable Home Screen**: Reorder and hide content rows to personalize your experience
- **TV-Optimized UI**: Built specifically for Android TV with D-pad navigation and Leanback components
